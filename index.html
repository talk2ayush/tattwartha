Who We Are

Tattwartha Power specialises in delivering end-to-end electronic design and component solutions that help customers bring their ideas to life with precision, reliability and speed.

We understand that every great product begins with strong foundations in design. That's why we offer comprehensive design-to-execution services — ensuring customers can transform concepts into working prototypes and market-ready products. From circuit design and PCB layout to Gerber file preparation, our expert team provides technical excellence and attention to detail at every step.

In addition to design services, we act as a one-stop destination for electronic components. Leveraging our industry connections and sourcing expertise, we supply a wide range of quality components tailored to our customers' requirements — whether for prototyping, small-batch production, or large-scale manufacturing.

Our Mission

To become a trusted partner for businesses and innovators by offering seamless electronic design services and dependable component supply under one roof. At Tattwartha Power, we don't just provide solutions — we collaborate, innovate, and grow with our customers.
